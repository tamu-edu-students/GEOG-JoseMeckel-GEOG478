About Us


Our Mission:
ProxiStorm's mission is to bring essential life-saving information to US residents, alerting them of the potential flood risks near them. Flooding can cause catastrophic damage to infrastructure, vegetation, and people's well-being. Proxistorm aims to give people an accurate idea of how far they are from floods using a risk assessment with three ranges of risk, based on live location and using flood history information.
